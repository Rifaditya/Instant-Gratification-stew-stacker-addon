## 🍲 Core Subsystem Highlights

1. **Full Stew & Soup Coverage**:
   Applies configurable stack limits to Mushroom Stew, Rabbit Stew, Beetroot Soup, and Suspicious Stew.

2. **Clean Bowl Return Logistics**:
   Consuming stews smoothly returns wooden bowls to player inventory or safely drops them to the world when full.

3. **Dynamic GameRule Plumbing**:
   Full in-game mutation without restart via `stew-stacker-addon:stew_limit`.

4. **Authoritative Addon Coordination**:
   Seamlessly integrates with Stack Size Adjuster and coordinates with sister addons.
